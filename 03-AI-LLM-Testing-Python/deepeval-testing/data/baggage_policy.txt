
SKYFLY AIRLINE BAGGAGE POLICY

1. Domestic check-in baggage allowance is 15 kg.

2. International check-in baggage allowance is 23 kg.

3. Cabin baggage allowance is 7 kg.

4. Passengers can carry one small laptop bag
   as a personal item.

5. Additional baggage costs Rs. 500 per kg.

6. Dangerous goods are not permitted.

7. Spare lithium batteries must be carried
   in cabin baggage.

8. Sports equipment is accepted subject to
   additional charges.

9. Lost baggage must be reported at the
   airport baggage desk.
