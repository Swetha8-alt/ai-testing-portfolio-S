
SKYFLY AIRLINE - CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY

1. Ticket Cancellation:
Passengers can cancel their flight tickets before departure.

2. Cancellation Fee:
The cancellation fee is Rs. 1000 per passenger.

3. Refund Processing:
Eligible refunds are processed within 7 working days.

4. Non-Refundable Tickets:
Non-refundable tickets are not eligible for a full refund.

5. Refund Eligibility:
Refund eligibility depends on the ticket type and applicable cancellation charges.

6. Passenger Name Change:
The available policy does not specify whether passenger names can be changed.
